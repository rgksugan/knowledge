# Walking the Western Ghats
by [[A.J.T. Johnsingh]]

## Highlights
> Roads are the greatest enemies of the forests.

## The Book in 3 Sentences
The author journeys through the Western Ghats, starting from the southern end and travelling all the way north to the Sahyadris. He recounts the treks he did with the Forest Department across different parts of the Ghats, along with his experiences staying in jungle lodges and spending time in the forests. It mixes a travel account of the places, forests and trekking experiences with academic detail on the plants and birds of the region.
