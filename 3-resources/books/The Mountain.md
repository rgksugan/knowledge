# The Mountain
by [[Ed Viesturs]]

## Highlights
> Reaching the summit is optional. Getting down is mandatory.

> Listen to the mountain. You can always come back. It will still be there.

> Having now felt the tug of war between ambition and common sense firsthand. I could see how in many cases ambition wins out.

> When the going gets tough, the weak make excuses.

> At Gorak Shep, only a day's march short of base camp, Paula was eating a bowl of rice for dinner when she found a huge ried spider in it. She plucked it out, tossed it aside, said "Yuck!" and just kept eating. I knew then that she was my kind of partner!

> There's always a temptation to follow the crowd, out of a nagging fear that they know something you don't, but in the end, one's instincts are the best guide.

> The summit is only the half way point.
